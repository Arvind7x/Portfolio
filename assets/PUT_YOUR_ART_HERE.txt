Put your art here.

Examples:
  assets/meme-1.png
  assets/poster-1.jpg
  assets/me.jpg   <- your photo (shows on home top)

Then in admin.html paste the path into Image, or in content.js set:
  img: "assets/meme-1.png"

Tips:
- .png / .jpg / .webp / .gif all work
- Keep each under ~500KB (use tinypng.com if big)
- Simple names, no spaces: meme-1.png (not "My Meme!! 1.png")
